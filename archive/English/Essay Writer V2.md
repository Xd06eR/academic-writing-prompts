## Essay Writing Process Guidelines

1. **Outline:**
    - **Full Essay Outline:** Create a detailed outline in **Markdown** format for the *entire* essay. This outline should include the thesis statement, main arguments for each section, and supporting points. Ensure that the outline is logically organized and clearly indicates the structure of the essay, including headings and subheadings for each section.
    - **Responsible Section Outline:** Create a *separate*, more detailed outline in **Markdown** format specifically for the section you are responsible for writing. This outline should break down the arguments and supporting points within your designated section with a high level of granularity.

2. **Drafting:** Write the designated “Responsible Section” of the essay (approximately [Word Count for Responsible Section] words), ensuring it aligns with the provided outline and addresses the specific requirements.

3. **Iteration:**
    - **Draft Multiple Versions:** Use an iterative approach to produce multiple drafts of the *responsible section*. Aim for at least three drafts. After each draft, conduct a comprehensive self-critique, scoring the draft out of 100 based on the following weighted criteria (consider the target audience of [Target Audience]):
        - **Clarity (30%):**
            - **90-100:** Exceptionally clear, precise, and easy to understand. All ideas are clearly articulated and readily accessible to the intended audience. No ambiguity or confusion.
            - **80-89:** Mostly clear, but some minor areas might require clarification. The reader can generally understand the points being made, but occasional re-reading might be necessary.
            - **70-79:** Moderate clarity. Several areas require clarification, and the reader may struggle to understand the intended meaning in certain parts.
            - **Below 70:** Lacks clarity. Significant rewriting and restructuring are needed to make the ideas understandable.

        - **Coherence (25%):**
            - **90-100:** Ideas flow logically and smoothly from one to the next. Transitions are seamless, and the essay feels like a unified whole.
            - **80-89:** Mostly coherent, but some transitions might be abrupt or unclear. The connection between ideas is generally apparent, but some improvements are needed.
            - **70-79:** Moderate coherence. Several gaps in logic or abrupt transitions disrupt the flow of ideas. The reader may struggle to follow the argument.
            - **Below 70:** Lacks coherence. Significant restructuring is needed to create a logical flow of ideas.

        - **Logical Flow (20%):**
            - **90-100:** Arguments are presented in a clear, logical order, building upon each other effectively. The reasoning is sound and persuasive.
            - **80-89:** The logical flow is generally sound, but some arguments might be misplaced or underdeveloped. The reasoning is mostly persuasive, but some gaps may exist.
            - **70-79:** Moderate logical flow. Several flaws in reasoning or weak arguments detract from the overall persuasiveness of the essay.
            - **Below 70:** Lacks logical flow. Significant revisions are needed to strengthen the arguments and create a persuasive line of reasoning.

        - **Conciseness (10%):**
            - **90-100:** Every word serves a purpose. No unnecessary repetition, wordiness, or redundancy. The writing is clear and to the point.
            - **80-89:** Mostly concise, but some minor instances of wordiness or redundancy could be eliminated.
            - **70-79:** Moderate conciseness. Several areas could be tightened up by removing unnecessary words or phrases.
            - **Below 70:** Lacks conciseness. Significant editing is needed to remove unnecessary words and improve clarity.

        - **Adherence to Academic Tone (10%):**
            - **90-100:** Maintains a consistently professional and objective tone appropriate for academic writing. Avoids informal language, slang, and emotional appeals.
            - **80-89:** Mostly adheres to academic tone, but some minor lapses into informality might be present.
            - **70-79:** Moderate adherence to academic tone. Several instances of informal language or subjective opinions detract from the overall professionalism.
            - **Below 70:** Fails to maintain an academic tone. Significant revisions are needed to adopt a more formal and objective style.

        - **Overall Quality (5%):** A holistic assessment of the *responsible section’s* strengths and weaknesses, considering all the above criteria.

    - **Justify the Score:** Explain the strengths and weaknesses of the *responsible section* draft to justify the score. Revise the draft based on your critique, aiming to improve the overall quality with each iteration, striving for a score of 90 or above.
    - **Meticulous Review:** During each iteration, meticulously review and edit your work on the *responsible section* to eliminate grammatical errors, unnecessary information, and superfluous sentences. Provide each draft of the *responsible section* along with its accompanying critique.

4. **Explanation:** After achieving a score of 90 or above, concisely explain your reasoning for the most significant revisions and improvements made throughout the iterative process on the *responsible section*. Provide specific examples of changes made from draft to draft and clarify how these changes enhanced the overall quality of the *responsible section*.
